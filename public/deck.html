01 · The Problem for Solo On-Chain Quant Desks

Basis Traders Fly Blind Between Orderbook Depth & Institutional ETF Flows

Running a one-person delta-neutral carry desk on decentralized perpetuals fails for three measurable reasons:

1. Headline APR Mirage

Raw perp funding looks attractive until you account for real L2 orderbook walking slippage, perp/spot fees, and maker/taker spread across both legs.

2. Macro Regime Blindness

Funding flips violently when US BTC/ETH Spot ETF net inflows swing (e.g. BTC z-score -1.31 vs +$110.3M daily print) or sector rotations hit SSI indices.

3. No Auditable Risk Gate

Solo traders lack institutional pre-trade VaR/CVaR stress testing, kill-switches, and verifiable API telemetry before sizing capital.
