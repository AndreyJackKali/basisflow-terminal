Institutional Data-Quality Guardrails

  • THIN BOOK (<$1M 24h vol): Raised from $100k to $1M after auditing real SoDEX data where a $382k micro-cap printed +448% carry. Thin markets are capped at conviction 55 and excluded from Peak Tradable Carry.
